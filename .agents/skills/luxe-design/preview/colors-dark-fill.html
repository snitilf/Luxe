The dark fill
--dark-fill primary button
--dark-fill-hover hover
--ink-1 type, not fills
--disabled-fill disabled

The cocoa fill carries the primary button, the user chat bubble, the annotate toggle track when it is on, and tooltip backgrounds. It is deliberately not the body ink sitting beside it: type and filled surfaces are different jobs, and collapsing them into one value is what made the primary button read as near-black. The pair that goes on it is always --dark-fill-text.
