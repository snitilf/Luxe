Categorical slots
--chart-1 through --chart-8
Load-bearing rule. Three slots sit below 3:1 against the canvas, so every chart carries direct labels, printed values, or a table view beside it. A legend alone is not sufficient. Build that into the chart component; do not leave it to whoever authors the page.

The order is fixed. It is the colour-blind safety mechanism, not decoration, so slots are assigned from one upwards and never cycled or reshuffled. Adjacent pairs are safe for lines, stacked bars and grouped bars, so all eight are available there. All-pairs separation holds for four, so scatter, bubble, choropleth and small multiples cap at four series. Marks: --chart-stroke strokes with round caps and joins, --chart-dot end dots ringed --chart-dot-ring in the canvas, gridlines in --hair, axis text in --ink-2 at label size, and a --chart-stack-gap surface gap between stacked segments.
