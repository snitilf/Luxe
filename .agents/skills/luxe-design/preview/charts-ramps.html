Ramps encode magnitude, categorical slots encode identity. Never mix them in one chart, and never build a ramp by tinting a categorical slot. The sequential ramp is monotonic in lightness, so it survives greyscale printing. The diverging midpoint is the system's warm grey, so no change reads as paper rather than as a colour. Neither ramp touches the status tokens, and neither has been exercised in a shipped heatmap yet, so check a real render before trusting the middle steps.
