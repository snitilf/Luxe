--font-mono

Two families, both bundled as latin-subset woff2 and served from the tool itself. Not a CDN: the tool has to work offline, and a published artifact may block external hosts. There is deliberately no serif and no third family. The sans carries --tracking-sans; the mono carries --tracking-mono, because negative tracking fights a monospace face's own metrics.
