--radius-nav
toolbar, inputs, chips
--radius-inner
banners, code, node cards
--radius-card
cards, panels, modals
--radius-bubble
speaker corner tightened
--radius-pill
CTAs, chips, swatches

Nest inward: an inner radius inside a card radius, never the other way round. The bubble radius is the card radius with the speaker-side bottom corner tightened, which is what makes a bubble point at its author without needing a tail. There is no other radius in the system; if a shape seems to want one, it wants a different component.
