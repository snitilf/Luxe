Buttons

One primary per surface, in the cocoa fill; hover moves to --dark-fill-hover. Everything else is the ghost: top plane, emphasis border, body ink. Destructive actions carry no colour of their own - ending a session is irreversible, but it is not a status, and colour in this system is spent on charts, status and the annotation stroke. A destructive action is a ghost button with a plain sentence under it saying what it does.

Focus is the info-slate ring at --focus-ring-width with --focus-ring-offset, never the gold, so a focused button never reads as an annotated one.
