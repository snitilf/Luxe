Resting
A card inside a panel. Border, no shadow.
Floating
A modal. One soft shadow, and only because it sits over the scrim.
--shadow-modal over --scrim

Resting surfaces never carry a shadow. Depth comes from the three planes plus hairlines, which is what keeps a dense page calm on a warm ground where shadows go muddy. Exactly one shadow token exists, and it belongs to the surface that genuinely floats above a scrim. Do not add a shadow to a button, a card, a bubble, or a toolbar; if something needs to read as raised, move it up a plane.
