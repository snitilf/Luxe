--text-heading / 500
Body copy, the reading size.
--text-body / 400
Controls, buttons, bubbles
--text-control / 400 or 500
Labels, metadata, axis text, chips
--text-label / 400 or 500
Micro-label
--text-label / 500 / --tracking-micro

Four sizes. Nothing between them, nothing above the heading. Two weights: regular and medium. No bold, no light. Emphasis is weight 500 or a size already in the scale, never a heavier face. Uppercase belongs to micro-labels alone and takes the positive tracking token, because uppercase closes up. Headings and body are never uppercase.
