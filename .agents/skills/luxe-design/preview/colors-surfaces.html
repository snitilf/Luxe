Surfaces
--canvas page, artifact
--surface-1 toolbar, panels
--surface-2 cards, inputs
--hair 1px rules
--strong emphasis rules

Hierarchy comes from three planes plus hairlines, not from shadows. Warm ivory holds the page, the toolbar sits a shade lighter, and cards go to the top plane. A card resting inside a panel gets a border, never a drop shadow. Do not invent a fourth plane: if two things need separating and the planes are used up, separate them with a hairline or with space.
