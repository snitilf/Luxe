Body, headings and values. --ink-1
Labels, metadata and axis text. --ink-2
Decoration only. --ink-3
Load-bearing rule. The third ink sits below the 4.5:1 text floor. It may tint a divider, a disabled label or a tick mark. It must never carry a value, a caption, a unit note or anything a reader needs. If you are reaching for it on real text, the answer is the second ink.

The first two inks clear the text floor on every surface plane in the system, so no combination of plane and ink needs checking case by case.
