The toolbar sits on the middle plane with a hairline underneath. The file name is display-only and truncates; the full path with a copy affordance lives in the overflow menu. The annotate toggle track is the cocoa fill when on and --toggle-track-off when off, with an ivory knob either way. When the session has ended the whole bar drops to --ended-opacity, except the ended chip, which keeps its specified contrast.
