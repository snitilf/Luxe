Status always ships with an icon and a text label, never colour alone. Each role is a triple: foreground on its own tint, inside its own border. Error shares a family with the warm chart slots, so the separation is carried by the lightness register, the tinted container and the icon-plus-label rule - and by the rule that a chart series which itself means good or bad wears status tokens rather than a categorical slot.
